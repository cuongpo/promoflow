# AI Builder Cup 2026 — research brief

**Last checked:** 4 October 2026 (Vietnam time). **Official site:** [aibuildercup.com](https://aibuildercup.com/). **Organizer:** Hack2skill. **Sponsor:** Google Cloud. The event is run independently by Hack2skill; Google Cloud supplies sponsorship, technical expertise, and educational resources. It is a JAPAC (Japan and Asia-Pacific) competition for professional builders to create a working AI prototype on Google Cloud, with an in-person final in Singapore. [Official overview](https://aibuildercup.com/)

## At a glance

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Entry | Free at every stage. |
| Team | 2–4 members. You can register alone, but must form or join a team by **11 October 2026**. |
| Eligibility | Each member must be **21 or older**, a working professional, entrepreneur, or startup participant, and physically based in and a legal resident of a JAPAC country. Full-time and part-time students are ineligible. Cross-country JAPAC teams are allowed. |
| Build | One fresh, working, end-to-end AI prototype in one of six themes, built primarily on Google Cloud and deployed via Cloud Run/GCP/Firebase. |
| Submission | Live deployed URL, public GitHub source repository, video **strictly under 3 minutes**, and a presentation deck; the challenge page says to convert the deck to PDF. Materials must be in English. |
| Cash prizes | **USD 30,000** total across seven listed awards. |
| Final | Shortlisted teams demo in Singapore on **4 December 2026**. Only two people per team may attend. |

Sources: [overview](https://aibuildercup.com/), [challenges and requirements](https://aibuildercup.com/themes.html), [FAQ](https://aibuildercup.com/Faqs.html), [terms](https://docs.google.com/document/d/e/2PACX-1vRm7ChZ6Ij9fG7uFDkxzUMpwgVeBmnQ6cMDnAIEEX84AiLBOOQ9cYbl3S5OzFBbcVb8TF55s-eVpiXb/pub), [prizes](https://aibuildercup.com/rewards.html).

## Eligibility and team rules

- The event is for **working professionals, entrepreneurs, and startups**, with a minimum age of 21. Students, including part-time students, cannot participate. If even one team member is a student, the entire team can be disqualified, including after selection for the final. Organizers may verify government ID, age, and professional status at any stage. [Overview](https://aibuildercup.com/) · [Terms](https://docs.google.com/document/d/e/2PACX-1vRm7ChZ6Ij9fG7uFDkxzUMpwgVeBmnQ6cMDnAIEEX84AiLBOOQ9cYbl3S5OzFBbcVb8TF55s-eVpiXb/pub)
- All members must be **physically based in and legal residents of JAPAC countries**. Members can live in different JAPAC countries. The published rules do not provide an exhaustive country list, so anyone with an uncertain residency situation should confirm with the organizer. [FAQ](https://aibuildercup.com/Faqs.html) · [Terms](https://docs.google.com/document/d/e/2PACX-1vRm7ChZ6Ij9fG7uFDkxzUMpwgVeBmnQ6cMDnAIEEX84AiLBOOQ9cYbl3S5OzFBbcVb8TF55s-eVpiXb/pub)
- Registering solo is allowed, **competing solo is not**. A team must have 2–4 people. Additions and replacements are allowed only through **11 October**; the terms say no roster changes after that date. One person may be on only one team, and each team may submit only one solution under one theme. [FAQ](https://aibuildercup.com/Faqs.html) · [Terms](https://docs.google.com/document/d/e/2PACX-1vRm7ChZ6Ij9fG7uFDkxzUMpwgVeBmnQ6cMDnAIEEX84AiLBOOQ9cYbl3S5OzFBbcVb8TF55s-eVpiXb/pub)
- Finalists must attend required checkpoints and respond on organizer-designated communication channels; failure may disqualify the team. The team leader is responsible for providing team-member details when requested. [Terms](https://docs.google.com/document/d/e/2PACX-1vRm7ChZ6Ij9fG7uFDkxzUMpwgVeBmnQ6cMDnAIEEX84AiLBOOQ9cYbl3S5OzFBbcVb8TF55s-eVpiXb/pub)

## Six challenge themes

| Theme | Examples named by the organizer |
| --- | --- |
| **BFSI: Intelligent Risk, Fraud & Financial Experiences** | Fraud and risk detection/mitigation, process automation, customer interaction, personalized financial experiences. |
| **Retail & Commerce: Intelligent Customer and Business Experiences** | Discovery, personalization, conversational shopping, demand planning, inventory, customer insights, fraud. |
| **Manufacturing: Intelligent Operations & Industrial Efficiency** | Efficiency, quality, reliability, supply and demand, sustainability; turning operational data into insights or actions. |
| **Media, Content & Digital Experiences** | Content creation, discovery, personalization, understanding, and experiences across media, entertainment, publishing, education, communication, and related ecosystems. |

| **Sustainability & Social Impact** | Resource efficiency, accessibility, resilience, community support, and sustainable decisions. |

## Build and submission requirements

1. **Start a fresh project during the official hackathon period.** The FAQ excludes projects already started; the terms require fresh code and original assets created during the official timeline and exclude pre-existing products, ongoing projects, or pre-launch code. Do not assume an existing product can be repackaged. [FAQ](https://aibuildercup.com/Faqs.html) · [Terms](https://docs.google.com/document/d/e/2PACX-1vRm7ChZ6Ij9fG7uFDkxzUMpwgVeBmnQ6cMDnAIEEX84AiLBOOQ9cYbl3S5OzFBbcVb8TF55s-eVpiXb/pub)
2. **Build a functional end-to-end prototype** that demonstrates the chosen solution. A pitch deck or static mockup alone is insufficient. The solution should have clear value, usable design, and a credible path to scale. [Challenge page](https://aibuildercup.com/themes.html) · [FAQ](https://aibuildercup.com/Faqs.html)
3. **Use Google's stack.** The challenge page requires Google AI models such as Gemini or Gemma **or** agentic platforms such as Agent Platform, Antigravity, or AI Studio, plus deployment on Google Cloud via Cloud Run or Firebase. The FAQ and terms say the prototype must be built primarily on Google Cloud; a solution built primarily on another cloud is ineligible. [Challenge page](https://aibuildercup.com/themes.html) · [Terms](https://docs.google.com/document/d/e/2PACX-1vRm7ChZ6Ij9fG7uFDkxzUMpwgVeBmnQ6cMDnAIEEX84AiLBOOQ9cYbl3S5OzFBbcVb8TF55s-eVpiXb/pub)
4. **Prepare all four mandatory artifacts:** (a) working public deployed URL; (b) **public GitHub repository** with source code; (c) public demo video **strictly under three minutes**—the challenge page suggests YouTube, Vimeo, or a public Google Drive link; (d) detailed presentation deck explaining the solution, architecture, business case, impact, feasibility, and scalability. The challenge page specifically asks for a PPT/deck converted to PDF. [Challenge page](https://aibuildercup.com/themes.html) · [FAQ](https://aibuildercup.com/Faqs.html) · [Terms](https://docs.google.com/document/d/e/2PACX-1vRm7ChZ6Ij9fG7uFDkxzUMpwgVeBmnQ6cMDnAIEEX84AiLBOOQ9cYbl3S5OzFBbcVb8TF55s-eVpiXb/pub)
5. **Use English** for code, documentation, and presentation materials. Identify the selected theme clearly. [Challenge page](https://aibuildercup.com/themes.html)

The official site says online program and problem-statement sessions will be available, but it gives no session schedule on the reviewed pages. [Challenge page](https://aibuildercup.com/themes.html) · [FAQ](https://aibuildercup.com/Faqs.html)

## Judging

All six themes use the same rubric. There is a scoring round before the final; shortlisted teams then demo live in Singapore. The names of the judges had not been published on the reviewed FAQ. [Judging page](https://aibuildercup.com/themes.html) · [FAQ](https://aibuildercup.com/Faqs.html)

| Criterion | Weight | What the rubric emphasizes |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Technical Merit & Gen AI Implementation | **40%** | Meaningful GenAI use, working and technically sound implementation, scalability and continued viability. |
| Problem Alignment & Impact | **25%** | Fit to the chosen theme and clear value for users or stakeholders. |
| Innovation & Creativity | **25%** | Originality, differentiation, and creative GenAI use beyond a superficial feature. |
| User Experience & Solution Design | **10%** | Usability, accessibility, and a cohesive experience. |

## Prizes and finalist travel

The seven awards listed on the [prizes page](https://aibuildercup.com/rewards.html) sum to **USD 30,000**:

| Award | Cash |
| --- | ---: |
| Winner | USD 10,000 |
| First runner-up | USD 7,000 |
| Second runner-up | USD 5,000 |
| Best use of Google Cloud AI tools | USD 2,000 |
| Most Impactful Solution | USD 2,000 |
| Social Choice Award | USD 2,000 |
| Best UI/UX | USD 2,000 |

Only **two representatives per finalist team** may travel to and attend the final, even if the team has three or four members. Additional teammates cannot attend at their own expense. The terms specify **economy flights, accommodation, and designated meals on the event day** for those two attendees. Visa application, fees, timing, and other ancillary costs are the attendees' responsibility. Travel bookings are non-transferable, and the organizer says it cannot substitute a teammate after a visa delay or refusal. [FAQ](https://aibuildercup.com/Faqs.html) · [Terms](https://docs.google.com/document/d/e/2PACX-1vRm7ChZ6Ij9fG7uFDkxzUMpwgVeBmnQ6cMDnAIEEX84AiLBOOQ9cYbl3S5OzFBbcVb8TF55s-eVpiXb/pub)

Prize recipients are responsible for applicable taxes. The terms say cash awards are paid within **60 days of the hackathon** and subject to tax deduction at source under applicable government rules. The organizer reserves the right to change prizes. [Terms](https://docs.google.com/document/d/e/2PACX-1vRm7ChZ6Ij9fG7uFDkxzUMpwgVeBmnQ6cMDnAIEEX84AiLBOOQ9cYbl3S5OzFBbcVb8TF55s-eVpiXb/pub)

Hack2skill also advertises **Google Cloud credits and mentorship**, including one-on-one mentorship after shortlisting. The reviewed event pages do not specify credit amounts or precise eligibility. ## Terms with practical consequences

- **Intellectual property:** For six months after the hackathon, the organizer has a right of first refusal to acquire an exclusive license to submitted materials. Before granting an exclusive sale, transfer, or license to a third party in that period, the participant must first offer the organizer the same terms; it has 30 days to accept. The terms also allow the organizer to publicize submitted ideas and grant the event/sponsors a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive promotional license for marketing content derived from entries. Anyone planning to commercialize the idea should read the exact wording. [Terms, “Intellectual Property Rights” and “Protection of Intellectual Property”](https://docs.google.com/document/d/e/2PACX-1vRm7ChZ6Ij9fG7uFDkxzUMpwgVeBmnQ6cMDnAIEEX84AiLBOOQ9cYbl3S5OzFBbcVb8TF55s-eVpiXb/pub)
- **Third-party material:** Entrants must have rights to submitted code, assets, data, and other material, and must avoid confidential or infringing content. The terms contain participant warranties and an indemnity concerning third-party rights. [Terms](https://docs.google.com/document/d/e/2PACX-1vRm7ChZ6Ij9fG7uFDkxzUMpwgVeBmnQ6cMDnAIEEX84AiLBOOQ9cYbl3S5OzFBbcVb8TF55s-eVpiXb/pub)
- **Rule changes and discretion:** The organizer may amend rules, dates, venue, judging criteria, and prizes, and its decisions are final under the terms. The terms say they prevail over inconsistent promotional material. [Terms](https://docs.google.com/document/d/e/2PACX-1vRm7ChZ6Ij9fG7uFDkxzUMpwgVeBmnQ6cMDnAIEEX84AiLBOOQ9cYbl3S5OzFBbcVb8TF55s-eVpiXb/pub)

### Visible inconsistencies on the official pages

1. The [challenge page](https://aibuildercup.com/themes.html) names six industry themes but its “Category Specification” and “Submission Specifics” text refers to **Healthcare, Education, Sustainability, Accessibility, Social Good** and a “social challenge.” This appears to be stale copy; the six theme headings and the [FAQ](https://aibuildercup.com/Faqs.html) agree on the current six-theme structure. Ask the organizer if the submission form lists something different.
2. The [challenge page](https://aibuildercup.com/themes.html) asks for a video of “3 minutes,” whereas the [FAQ](https://aibuildercup.com/Faqs.html) and [terms](https://docs.google.com/document/d/e/2PACX-1vRm7ChZ6Ij9fG7uFDkxzUMpwgVeBmnQ6cMDnAIEEX84AiLBOOQ9cYbl3S5OzFBbcVb8TF55s-eVpiXb/pub) say **under** three minutes. Follow the stricter rule.
3. The official [overview](https://aibuildercup.com/) schedules evaluation through **6 November**; a [Hack2skill social post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/hack2skill_one-build-one-roadmap-one-destination-activity-7504083512854208512-HeeF) says through **31 October**. Both identify **7 November** for finalist announcements.

The [prize page](https://aibuildercup.com/rewards.html) initially contains “USD 0” in its HTML because the amount animates from zero to **USD 30,000**; its accessibility label and the listed awards confirm the intended total. This is a display detail, not a prize discrepancy.
